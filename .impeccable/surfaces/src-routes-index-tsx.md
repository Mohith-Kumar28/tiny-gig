# Surface brief: TinyGig, whole product (landing, worker app, admin console)

Scope and mode: landing + /how-it-works + /faq are Persuade. Worker app (/w) and admin console (/admin) are Operate; the world lends them only type, palette, density and one signature move.

Audience and job: Indian workers with Reddit accounts on phones (apply, raise hand, post, prove, get paid by UPI); a small ops team at laptops (keep the account register true, pick accounts, review proof, pay weekly).

Proof and content: real mechanism only (10-minute interest window, staff selection, 24h live hold, insights screenshot, account-age levels, Monday UPI payout with UTR). No invented counts, testimonials or logos. Example gigs labelled as examples.

## Direction contract

THESIS: TinyGig is a khata: a register of accounts and of money owed, settled weekly. The surface refuses the category default (cream ground, orange pill CTA, tilted gig cards, bento of equal tiles, numbered step cards) and instead lays every page on ledger structure: a date column, a margin rule, particulars, and right-aligned rupees.

OWN-WORLD: bahi-khata red cloth as a full-strength flat field (header band, closing section, balance plate); cool white ledger paper with faint ballpoint-blue ruling; Reynolds-blue ink for links, selection and handwritten-feeling entries; carbon black type. Archivo variable: condensed caps for column heads and labels, normal width for UI, expanded heavy for display. Square-ish 4px corners, hairline rules, no soft card shadows. Raise from the Blue Note sleeve: red is committed as a whole field, never a sprinkled accent. Raise from the jackfield schedule: state is drawn by rule pattern too (single rule pending, double rule settled, strike-through cancelled), with colour only reinforcing it. Raise from the nixie counter: rupees live in fixed right-aligned tabular columns; numbers are the interface.

STORY: visitors see a real-looking page of a worker's ledger (labelled example), understand the gig to UPI loop with its actual timings, trust the honest rules, and apply.

FIRST VIEWPORT: red cloth header band with the white t-mark and Apply. Below, paper with blue ruling and a red margin rule. Left of the margin: small condensed dates. Headline set large across the particulars column; one line of subcopy; Apply + How it works. Right half: an example ledger page of one week's gigs (account, community, status by rule, ₹ amount) closing on a double-ruled total and a "Paid to UPI, UTR" line.
